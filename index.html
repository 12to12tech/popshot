QUESTIONS

Before you upload.

How does Popshot work?

Upload a talking-head clip, pick a caption style, and clean up the words in the transcript. Popshot suggests a hook line and builds thumbnail options, then renders a vertical short ready for TikTok, Reels or YouTube Shorts.

Does anything get uploaded to a server?

No. Transcription (Whisper), person segmentation and video rendering all run inside your browser. The only network traffic is downloading the AI models and fonts the first time.

Do I edit on a timeline?

There is no timeline. Every change happens in the transcript: correct a word or strike a filler and the caption track retimes itself automatically.

What if I change the words after picking a style?

Go ahead. The captions resync automatically so they always match the transcript.

How many caption styles are there?

30+ designed styles across Popular, Playful, Multiline, Dynamic, Editorial, Social, Neon & FX, Retro, Desi and Speakers — karaoke color-flips, box highlights, neon glows, kinetic squash-and-stretch, serif lockups and more. Each pairs a primary sentence font with an accent emphasis font.

What are behind-the-subject captions?

Caption words rendered behind the speaker for a layered depth look, using on-device person segmentation — live in the editor preview and identical in the export.

What resolution and format does it export?

1080×1920 vertical 9:16 by default (1080×1080 and 1920×1080 also available). Chrome exports H.264 MP4 directly; other browsers export WebM. The preview and the export render the exact same composition.

What kind of videos work best?

Short talking-head clips — you on camera, talking. Keep each clip under 5 minutes for snappy transcription.
