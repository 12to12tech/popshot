HOW IT WORKS

Four steps, no server.

1

Cut it out

On-device person segmentation, or grow a selection in from the border for flat and gradient backdrops. Touch up whatever it missed with a brush.

2

Tear the edge

The cut is traced into a real polygon, pushed outward along its own normals with layered noise and feathered with fibres, so the deckle stays crisp at any export size.

3

Move it

Ten paper animations — fold-outs, a rip reveal, an edge-on flip, a flutter loop, a stop-motion boil that re-cuts the torn edge on every frame — plus thirteen typography and scene effects in the Effects tab.

4

Take it away

Transparent PNG, animated GIF with optional 1-bit alpha, or an MP4/WebM on a green screen for keying in your editor.
